# Completed results: reading guide

The files under `results/reference/` are preserved numerical outputs, not results
generated by the release demo. The benchmark and exploratory width study have
separate statistical families and evidence chronology.

## Controlled PCA8 benchmark

At the primary training scale, PdrQC-Matched was the strongest quantum-derived
method across four datasets. Its exact primary metric was 0.7934 accuracy on
MNIST, 0.7718 on Fashion-MNIST, 0.6810 on CIFAR-10, and 0.78467 AUROC on
BreastMNIST.

The matched C-RBF control exceeded it by 0.0219 accuracy on Fashion-MNIST and
0.0350 on CIFAR-10; both contrasts survived the global twelve-test Holm
correction. The BreastMNIST difference was not statistically detectable.
This is not evidence of equivalence. The unmatched 512D C-ResNet reference was
strongest overall.

The completed benchmark includes exact inference, finite-shot sampling,
data scaling, calibration, selective risk and explicit resource boundaries.
Read the [original summary](published/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md),
[execution status](published/FINAL_EXPERIMENT_STATUS.md), and
[failure summary](published/FAILURE_SUMMARY.md) together.

## Significance, not just point estimates

| Analysis | Location | Family |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Primary PCA8 comparisons | `results/reference/benchmark/statistics/primary_tests.csv` | Global 12-test Holm |
| Calibration/selective risk | `results/reference/benchmark/statistics/uncertainty_tests.csv` | Separate 6-test Holm |
| Bootstrap intervals | `results/reference/benchmark/statistics/bootstrap_intervals.csv` | 10,000 replicates |
| Scaling ranking intervals | `results/reference/benchmark/statistics/data_scaling_intervals.csv` | Reported separately |
| PCA16 versus PCA8 | `results/reference/width/statistics/exploratory_tests.csv` | Exploratory 2-test Holm |

Eight primary tests and four uncertainty tests survived their respective
corrections. The two width tests both have Holm p=0.0543945605 and are not
significant at 0.05. Do not relabel the post-hoc width extension as prospective.

## Claim boundary

The evidence does not establish quantum advantage, clinical superiority,
hardware efficiency, or a causal advantage from entanglement. More qubits did
not establish a reliable improvement. All quantum predictive outputs came from
exact statevectors and offline sampling; QPU shots were zero.

The selected figures in `docs/figures/` are original exported figure files.
The figure-rendering utility produces a separate layout from their saved CSV
values without changing point estimates or intervals.
